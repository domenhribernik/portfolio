Scope: views/peal, the whole surface. Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience: a portfolio visitor who has never heard of change ringing, and the
smaller number who have. Job: hear that a compact string of place notation
generates hundreds of unique rows and comes home exactly to rounds, then take a
rope and find out how hard it is to put a bell where it belongs. Action: press

## Direction contract

THESIS  The page is not paper, it is the bell. A founder's measured drawing
scribed into oxidised bronze, where the metal's own two states carry every bit
of state the product has: cold at rest, hot when it sounds. It refuses both
pages this subject always gets, the parish noticeboard in cream and serif, and
the dark audio toy with glowing circles and a waveform.

OWN-WORLD  Three inks and nothing else. Patina (#2f5148) floods the sheet, with
a deeper patina for recessed wells and a lighter one for raised plates. Every
line, letterform, grid and figure is knocked out of it in warm bone (#efe7d6),
at draughting hairline weights of 0.5, 1 and 2px. Pour orange (#ff6a1f) is the
only hot ink and it means one thing, sound: a bell striking, the row sounding
now, the rope in your hands, your own line through the rows. No gradient, no
photograph, no fourth colour, and the treble is told apart from working bells by
line weight, the way a draughtsman does it, never by a second accent. Bevan sets
the cast lettering, Barlow Condensed the draughting labels, Azeret Mono every
figure and every row of digits. Corners are square. Nothing is elevated, because
nothing on a drawing sheet is.

RAISE (from the collider event display, declined)  Every figure is leadered to
the part it measures and never parked in a panel: the five partial frequencies
lead off the bell in section, place numbers sit on the rows, striking error in
milliseconds hangs off your own rope.

RAISE (from the one-bit desktop, declined)  State is drawn in metal, never in
opacity. At rest a thing is knocked out in bone; sounding, it floods with pour;
silent because a person has the rope, it is left as outline only; unavailable, it
carries the founder's cancel line struck through it.

RAISE (from the character reference sheet, declined)  Selecting a bell flashes
and holds every place it occupies at once, across the rows, the tower plan and
the section, so a control and its consequences are bound on screen.

RAISE (from the socialist kiosk print, declined)  The ink set is hard-limited and
policed. Three inks, so the hot one can only ever mean sound.

STORY  A visitor arrives to a machine already drawn and understands in one
viewport that this is a working thing, not an article. They press LOOK TO, hear
six bells fall down a scale, and watch a line cut itself through a column of
digits. They learn that the string in the title block generated all of it. Then
they take a rope, discover that a bell is three hundred milliseconds wide and
they are eighty of them late, and come back to get better.

FIRST VIEWPORT  Full bleed patina, no site navbar, no hero word. Left third: the
bell in section at about 70vh, drawn in bone hairlines, five leader lines running
right to its partial names and live frequencies; the section redraws to whichever
bell just struck, so the drawing is a readout. Centre: the rows, a scribed column
of Azeret digits falling downward, the sounding row carried on a bone rule that
steps rather than glides, the treble's path a 2px bone stroke and the taken
bell's a 2px pour stroke cut through the digits. Right: the tower plan, bells
round a circle in their current order, ropes and sallies drawn, the rope you hold
inked in pour. Along the bottom edge a ruled title block in the drawing-sheet
grammar: PEAL, the method, the stage, the place notation, changes and leads, and
the primary action, a wide LOOK TO, which is the command a conductor actually
gives. Below 900px it stacks: plan, rows, title block sticky at the foot, the
section reduced to its partial readout.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION  Taking a rope. Tap a bell in the tower plan and the
machine stops ringing it, the sheet re-inks around it, its line through the rows
turns pour, and the title block becomes a striking readout that fills in blow by
blow, each row gaining a small deviation bar drawn off the row rule, left for
quick and right for slow.

MOTION GRAMMAR  Nothing eases, because ringing is metrical. Every step is
quantised to the bell gap: the row rule steps one row per row, the plan reorders
on the row, the section cuts to the new bell on the blow. The single continuous
motion is the rope, whose sally rises and falls on the real handstroke and
backstroke period. A strike flash is instant on and decays over that bell's own
hum time, so the tenor's glow outlasts the treble's.

FORM  The Founder's Sheet: a bell foundry's measured drawing, scribed into the
metal it describes rather than printed on paper. Candidate 6 of seven on the
grounded list, assigned by concept seed f29e30aa. Code-led: no image generation
exists on this machine, so there is no comp and the ambition rides in this block.

DEVIATION FROM THE CONTRACT  MOTION GRAMMAR promised that the chamber plan
"reorders on the row". It does not, on purpose. In a real ringing chamber the
ropes hang in a fixed circle in bell order and never move; what changes every
row is the order in which they are pulled. So the built plan keeps the ropes
where they physically are and sends the strike travelling round the circle in
the row's own order, which is what rope sight actually is. Everything else in
the contract is built as written.

FINISH  unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance
